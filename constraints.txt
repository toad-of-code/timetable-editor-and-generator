═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
              TIMETABLE SOLVER — CONSTRAINT REFERENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

File: src/solver/constraints.ts
Solver: (1+1)-ES (Evolutionary Strategy)
Fitness = (Hard Violations × Hard Penalty) + (Gap Penalty × Gap Weight) + (Room Util Penalty × Util Weight)


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                        HARD CONSTRAINTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Hard violations MUST be exactly 0 for the timetable to be feasible.

  1. TIME BOUNDARY
     Function: countTimeBoundaryViolations()
     Rule:     A session must absolutely not exceed the final slot of the day.
               endSlot = startSlot + duration - 1 must be ≤ 8 (18:30).

  2. BREAK / LUNCH CROSSING
     Function: countBreakViolations()
     Rule:     A continuous multi-slot session (like a 2-hour lab) must NOT 
               span across a structural break.
               • Slot 2 → Slot 3  (10:50 – 11:00 break)
               • Slot 4 → Slot 5  (13:00 – 14:30 lunch)
               (Single-slot 1-hour sessions are naturally exempt).

  3. ROOM NON-OVERLAP
     Function: countRoomOverlaps()
     Rule:     A physical room can host at most ONE session at any (day, slot).
               Multiple sessions placed in the same room simultaneously cause clashes.

  4. PROFESSOR NON-OVERLAP
     Function: countProfessorOverlaps()
     Rule:     A distinct professor can teach at most ONE session at any (day, slot).

  5. STUDENT GROUP (SECTION) NON-OVERLAP
     Function: countGroupOverlaps()
     Rule:     A specific section of students can attend at most ONE session at 
               any given (day, slot).
     Exception: Concurrent sessions from different synced baskets are allowed here 
                because they are handled separately by the anti-clash rule below.

  6. ELECTIVE RULES & SYNCHRONIZATION
     Function: countElectiveSyncViolations()
     Rule A (Sync): All elective classes belonging to a predefined "Synced Basket" 
                    (e.g., HSMC or MDM) MUST be scheduled at the exact same (day, slot).
                    This allows students from different sections to scatter and 
                    attend the elective they chose.
     Rule B (Free): Electives in "Free Baskets" (basket-1, basket-2, etc.) can be
                    scheduled independently — some may overlap, others at different
                    times. The solver is free to spread them across slots for better
                    room utilization.
     Rule C (Anti-Clash): Sessions from DIFFERENT baskets (e.g., Basket-1 vs Basket-2) 
                          must never overlap. This guarantees a student can safely pick 
                          one subject from Basket 1 and another from Basket 2 without overlap.

  7. LAB ROOM FOR PRACTICALS
     Function: countLabRoomViolations()
     Rule:     If a sessions slot_type is 'Practical', it MUST be assigned to a room 
               where the room_type is explicitly 'Lab'. Normal classrooms are invalid.

  8. HOME ROOM FOR LECTURES/TUTORIALS
     Function: countHomeRoomViolations()
     Rule:     Core theoretical lectures and tutorials MUST occur in the designated 
               'Home Room' specifically assigned to that section/group. 
               (Practicals and Electives are exempt from this).

  9. WMC (WHOLE BATCH) VS SECTION
     Function: countWMCSectionOverlaps()
     Rule:     If a session is flagged as WMC (meaning the entire batch must attend, 
               like a mass guest lecture), no individual sub-section belonging to 
               that batch can have an independent class scheduled at that time.

  10. 2+1 LECTURE FORMAT
     Function: countTwoOneLectureViolations()
     Rule:     For core (non-elective) subjects with lectures >= 2, the lecture
               hours are split into:
                 • Exactly ONE 2-hour double-lecture block (lecturePairIndex = 0)
                 • All remaining hours as individual 1-hour sessions (lecturePairIndex = -1)
               Example breakdown:
                 lectures=2  →  one 2-hr session (total: 1 session)
                 lectures=3  →  one 2-hr + one 1-hr (total: 2 sessions)
                 lectures=4  →  one 2-hr + two 1-hr (total: 3 sessions)
               The constraint enforces that no two lecture sessions for the
               same subject+group fall on the same day. This guarantees the
               2-hr block and the 1-hr remainder(s) are spread across different
               days, preventing overloading a single day with the same subject.
               Elective lectures, tutorials, and practicals are NOT affected.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                        SOFT CONSTRAINTS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Soft penalties are optimized to make schedules nicer, but do not invalidate them.

  1. STUDENT GAP PENALTY (COMPRESSION)
     Function: computeGapPenalty()
     Rule:     The solver scans every student groups daily schedule from their first 
               arrival slot to their final departure slot. For every empty "free" slot 
               in between, a penalty is added. The solver actively tries to minimize this 
               to compress the students day and remove awkward waiting periods on campus.

  2. ROOM UTILIZATION PENALTY
     Function: computeRoomUtilizationPenalty()
     Rule:     For each session, the solver checks how well the assigned rooms capacity
               matches the number of students attending (studentCount / roomCapacity).
               — If the fill ratio drops below 60%, a graduated penalty is applied
                 (under-utilization: wasteful big room for small class)
               — If the fill ratio exceeds 120%, a graduated penalty is applied
                 (over-utilization: room too small for the class)
               Example (under): Room cap = 100, students = 10 → util = 10% → penalty = 50
               Example (over):  Room cap = 50, students = 80 → util = 160% → penalty = 40
     Config:   roomUtilizationWeight = 0.8
               roomUtilizationThreshold = 0.60 (60% minimum fill)
               roomOverutilizationThreshold = 1.20 (120% maximum fill)
     Note:     Locked sessions (from published timetables) are exempt.

  3. PROFESSOR SAME-HALF PENALTY
     Function: computeProfessorSameHalfPenalty()
     Rule:     For each professor on each day, the solver checks whether they
               have classes in BOTH the morning half (slots 1–4, up to 13:00)
               and the afternoon half (slots 5–8, from 14:30). If they do,
               a penalty of 1 is added for that (professor, day) pair.
               The solver tries to keep each professors daily sessions confined
               to a single half of the day.
     Config:   professorSameHalfWeight = 5.0
     Note:     Locked sessions are excluded from this check.

  Slot 3:  11:00 – 12:00
  Slot 4:  12:00 – 13:00

  Slot 5:  14:30 – 15:30
  Slot 6:  15:30 – 16:30
  Slot 7:  16:30 – 17:30
  Slot 8:  17:30 – 18:30

  Days: Mon–Fri (1–5)


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                      MUTATOR BEHAVIORS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • Mutate Time: Uses dynamic (1+1)-ES σ (sigma) via the 1/5th Rule context.
  • Sync Lock: Day, Time, and Relocate mutations forcibly drag entire synced 
    elective baskets with them to keep them synchronized.
  • Swap: Only allowed to swap core classes with identical durations. 
    Electives cannot be swapped.
  • Break Snap: Time mutations that push a class over a break boundary automatically 
    snap the class to the nearest safe valid slot.
